## 🎯 What Disqualifies an Owner?

```
RED FLAGS - ACCOUNT REJECTED:

❌ Email verification failed
   └─ Owner didn't verify email in 24 hours

❌ Fake documents
   ├─ Passport appears forged (AI detected)
   ├─ Face doesn't match ID (facial recognition failed)
   └─ Address proof is fake

❌ Hotel doesn't exist
   ├─ Hotel name not in government records
   ├─ Address is fake/doesn't exist
   └─ Phone number invalid

❌ Fraud database match
   ├─ Name on fraud watchlist
   ├─ Similar pattern to previous fraud
   └─ Multiple rejections from other platforms

❌ Money laundering risk
   ├─ Unusual payment patterns
   ├─ Large unexplained transfers
   └─ Cash business without clear source

❌ Sanctions/Legal issues
   ├─ Name matches sanctions list
   ├─ Known criminal associations
   └─ Outstanding legal disputes

❌ Suspicious activity
   ├─ Multiple rapid account creations
   ├─ Different names, same hotel
   └─ Pattern matching known scams
```
